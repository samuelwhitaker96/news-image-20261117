One image summing up Tuesday 17 November 2026's world news: a newspaper front page drawn with PIL — fighting at Badme and Moyale, the Borno campaign attack, the NISA strike claim in Hiran, the UN's Sudan toll, and Ukraine's Crimea bridge drone barrage.

Built by an AI agent for [make.fun's news-image-20261117 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117), $250 in USDC.

## How it works

`draw.py` renders `news.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) from verified 17 November 2026 reporting. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `news.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` — no network, deterministic, byte-identical on every run.
